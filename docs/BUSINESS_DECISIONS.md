# Business decisions (owner-confirmed, Oct 2026)

First target market: **UK**. Currency is detected automatically and buyers can switch; exchange rates update daily and automatically.

Source of truth for how the two seller models behave. Where code disagrees with this file, the code is wrong.
"Curated" = Curated by Solomon. "Marketplace" = a brand selling under its own name.

## 1. The two seller models

| Topic | Curated by Solomon | Marketplace brand |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant of record | Solomon Bharat | The brand. Buyer and brand transact; Solomon is the marketplace and charges commission |
| Seller type chosen by | Admin, at approval. The applicant never sees this choice | Same |
| Who sets buyer price | Admin, by hand, per product and tier | The brand |
| Agent price | Admin sets a fixed amount per product | Not applicable. Agents only buy curated products |
| Product approval | Required, and every edit goes back for re-approval | Not required, including edits |
| Name shown to buyers | "Solomon" | Brand name |
| What the seller sees | Only the products that were ordered from them. No buyer details | Full buyer and order details |
| Buyer messaging | Buyer ↔ Solomon team only | None. Buyer and brand do not message on-platform |
| Minimum order value | Not applicable | Brand must set a shop minimum, plus MOQ per product |
| Shipping | Solomon collects at seller's door → Jaipur warehouse → consolidated DDP to buyer | Brand ships direct to buyer. Brand chooses: own rates, or Solomon's shipping service (rates via partner API) |
| Dispatch proof | Solomon attaches photos; seller may also upload | Seller must upload photos |
| Export documents | Generated by Solomon for the order | Generated in the seller portal |
| Seller payout | On dispatch | On dispatch. Instant if the buyer is the brand's own customer |
| Commission | None (Solomon earns the margin) | 25% first order per buyer, 15% repeat. **0% for the brand's own buyers.** Charged on order value only, never on shipping |

## 2. Sellers
- One seller account = one brand. Multiple team members per seller are allowed.
- Individual artisans without GST can sell only as Curated.
- Verification: product quality, online and offline presence, sometimes a physical visit.
- Both types can switch products on/off and go on vacation without approval.
- Solomon keeps an internal seller rating (not shown publicly).
- Sellers sign a digital seller agreement in-platform (to be drafted).
- Must track for every buyer whether they are Solomon's buyer or the brand's own buyer (drives 0% commission and instant payout).

## 3. Product listing
Mandatory: title, description, material, craft technique, region, dimensions, weight, colour, variants (each with own price/MOQ/stock), MOQ, HSN code, lead time, made-to-order or ready stock, SKU, inventory quantity, care instructions. Minimum 2 high-quality photos.
Recommended: video, the story of the craft.
- Samples: Marketplace brands must state whether they offer samples, the max sample quantity and the sample price. Curated: admin may set a sample price. Buyers can order samples from the listing.
- No buyer customisation on standard listings; every listing has a "Custom requirement" button (RFQ).
- AI-assisted copy writing for sellers.
- Category tree: curated Indian-craft taxonomy based on iTokri and Amazon.

## 4. Buyers
- Business verification: not now, planned later.
- Prices visible after sign-up.
- See prices in, and pay in, their local currency. FX risk sits with the seller.
- Team accounts and multiple addresses allowed. No VIP/custom price lists.
- Site-wide RFQ form.
- Payment: 100% advance. Payment gateway pending (PayPal not live yet).
- Cancellation: allowed until the order is confirmed (Curated) or accepted by the brand (Marketplace). Unfulfillable orders are refunded.

## 5. Agents
- Indian or overseas resellers who sell to D2C brands, boutiques and small buyers. No territories, no targets.
- Buy curated products at the admin-set agent price. Can ship to a named end customer.
- Catalogue PDF is **white-label**: no Solomon Bharat branding, no prices, and it carries the agent's company name, phone and email.
- No referral commission for now.

## 6. Admin and compliance
- Single admin role for now; all pricing, approvals, refunds and payouts are admin-only.
- Full audit log is required. Reports for everything needed for auditing.
- Admin can place orders on behalf of buyers.
- Notifications by email and WhatsApp. English only.
- Legal entity: India. GST/IEC/LUT compliance is in progress.
- Needed: Terms, Privacy, Seller Agreement, GDPR cookie consent. No return or damage policy yet (claims decided on photo evidence; shipping insurance planned).
- Mobile-friendly site. Shopify/WooCommerce import is one-time. Google Analytics. Free hosting tier for now.
- Benchmarks: Faire and Ankorstore.

## 7. Follow-up decisions (round 2)
- Brand's own buyers join through the brand's **personal invite link** (`/invite/<code>`). Only buyers with no earlier paid order from that brand can be claimed.
- Prices are shown and charged in the buyer's currency, with automatic daily rates. Agent prices are converted the same way.
- Until the payment gateway is live, payment is **manual bank transfer**: the admin confirms receipt with the UTR (`PAYMENT_MODE=manual`).
- Marketplace brands have **24 hours** from payment to accept an order, or it is cancelled and refunded.
- Solomon collects payment for marketplace orders too, and pays the brand net of commission.
- Legal entity: **Solomon Bharat Private Limited, Jaipur, India**. Terms, Privacy, Cookie Policy and Seller Agreement are published on the site.
- Custom requirements (RFQs) always go to the Solomon team.
- Samples: not yet.

## 8. Order lifecycle (as built)
- Curated: Awaiting payment → Payment received → Confirmed → Procuring → At warehouse (Jaipur) → Quality checked (photos) → Dispatched → Delivered. Seller paid on dispatch.
- Marketplace: Awaiting payment → Awaiting brand acceptance (24h) → Accepted → Dispatched (photos + tracking; brand paid) → Delivered. Own-buyer orders are paid out on payment.
- Buyer can cancel until Confirmed/Accepted. Cancelled paid orders are marked Refunded by an admin with a reference.

## 9. Round 3 decisions (as built)
- Damage claims: report within 7 days of delivery, with photos.
- Refunds after a brand declines or misses the 24h window wait for admin approval (admin records the refund reference).
- Buyers see their custom requests under Account → Custom requests.
- Brands can add their own customers by email (linked now, or on sign-up).
- Applicants no longer pick a seller type; the admin assigns Curated or Marketplace at approval. Applicants accept the versioned Seller Agreement.
- Products require craft technique, region, made-to-order/ready stock, lead time and care instructions.
- Sellers have vacation mode; admins keep a private 1-5 internal rating per seller.
- Commercial invoice and packing list PDFs per order (admin: all; brand: own).
- Notifications are also sent on WhatsApp (Twilio) once configured.
- "Draft with AI" product descriptions once ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is configured.

## 10. Not built yet (needs input or a provider)
- Payment gateway (waiting on the provider; manual bank transfer is live meanwhile).
- Shipping rates: curated needs the shipping partner's API; marketplace brands choosing own rates vs Solomon's service.
- Seller team members (several logins per seller).
- Buyer business verification (deferred).
- GST TCS / TDS (194-O) computation and certificates for marketplace sellers.

## 11. Samples (as built)
- Marketplace brands must say on every product whether they offer samples; if yes, the maximum sample quantity and the price per sample unit. Admins can set the same for curated products.
- Buyers see "Samples available" on the product page (price after sign-up) and use "Order a sample". It arrives in the admin's Custom Requirements inbox marked "Sample order"; the team emails the total with shipping and bank details.

## 12. Round 4 decisions (15 Oct 2026, as built)
- Product pages show every detail the seller enters (including packaging) in a table, plus the craft story and, for brands, the brand story.
- Buyers choose "Wholesale order" or "Order a sample" on the product page.
- Marketplace brands must enter their minimum order value in a pop-up as soon as they sign in.
- "Browse products" opens an all-products page (`/products`).
- The sign-up page uses a designed pattern/map panel, not a photo.
- Samples can be ordered directly like a wholesale order (cart → checkout). Sample lines use the sample price, ignore MOQ and the brand's minimum order value, and are capped at the sample limit.
- Admins can switch an approved seller between Curated and Marketplace at any time (not while orders are in progress).

## 13. Solomon AI (15 Oct 2026)
- Public AI guide at /guide; visitors must give name, email and phone before chatting; no account needed.
- The AI learns from what the admin uploads (PDF, images, spreadsheets, text), answers in its own words, and uses web search when needed (on by default, switchable in admin).
- The admin section suggests what to upload next.
- (16 Oct 2026) Solomon AI starts with 4 tap-to-answer questions (what they do, who they sell to, categories, selling price), then recommends 3 products at a time as cards with photo, story, why it fits, and an estimated cost breakdown (India price, shipping, duty and VAT, landed cost, suggested retail), followed by short advice on who to target, how to position and where to sell. Visitors can like and save products into a shortlist.
- (16 Oct 2026) Exception to "prices visible after sign-up": Solomon AI may show an indicative "from ₹X" buyer price for catalogue products to visitors who have given their name, email and phone to start the chat. Shipping, duty and landed figures are marked as estimates.
- (16 Oct 2026) Solomon AI does not promote Solomon Bharat; it shows the WhatsApp button to Pranjal (wa.me/919588007925) only when the visitor shows buying intent.
- (17 Oct 2026) Approval emails carry a one-time "Set your password and sign in" link valid for 7 days (never the password in a link); temporary password kept as a fallback.
- (17 Oct 2026) Admins are emailed for every paid order; marketplace brands get "New order — please accept". Curated sellers are not emailed (orders go through admin) unless the owner asks for it.
- (17 Oct 2026) Listing: made-to-order products need no inventory; ready-stock products need stock of at least 1. Dimensions (with unit cm/inch/m) and HSN code are required for new listings.
